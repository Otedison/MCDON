Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book MC Don for an event?

You can book me by filling out the contact form above, emailing me directly at contact@mcdoncorporate.com, or calling +254 712 345 678. Please provide event details including date, location, type of event, and estimated number of guests.

What types of events do you host?

I specialize in corporate events including conferences, award ceremonies, product launches, team building events, AGMs, and client appreciation events. I also host private events such as weddings, birthdays, and galas.

Do you travel for events outside Nairobi?

Yes, I'm available for events across Kenya and internationally. Travel expenses are discussed during the booking process and included in the final quote.

How far in advance should I book?

I recommend booking at least 3-6 months in advance for major events. However, I do accommodate last-minute bookings when my schedule permits. Contact me to check availability.

Where can I buy MC Don merchandise?

You can purchase official MC Don merchandise through our online store at donproductions.hustlesasa.shop. We offer a variety of products including apparel and accessories.
